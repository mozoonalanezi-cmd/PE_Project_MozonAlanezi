# TC-Rules-v1_2026_10_15

Responsible-use rules for the Learner Support Desk at a Training Center

1. Data
Before pasting a learner request into an AI tool, the staff member replaces every name, ID number, and phone number with a code.

2. Verification
Before sending a reply, the staff member checks it with the five-point verification checklist and sends only replies that score 5 out of 5.

3. Fairness
Before a template is added to the library, it must pass the swap test with no unexplained difference in tone, length, facts, or next step.

4. Policy
When preparing a reply, the staff member uses only approved Library Facts and approved official channels for information or referrals.

5. Human decision
Before a reply is sent to a learner, a staff member reviews and approves the final reply.
